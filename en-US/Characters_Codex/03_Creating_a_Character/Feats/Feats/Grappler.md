# Grappler

**Prerequisite:**
Strength 13 or higher

* You gain advantage on attack rolls against a creature you are grappling.
You can use your action to try to pin a creature you are grappling. Make a Strength (Athletics) check contested by its Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). If you succeed, you and the creature are both restrained until the grapple ends.

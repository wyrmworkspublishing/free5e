# Grappler

**Prerequisite:**
Strength 13 or higher

* You gain advantage on attack rolls against a creature you are grappling.
* You can use your action to try to pin a creature you are grappling. Make a Strength (_Athletics_) check contested by its Strength (_Athletics_) or Dexterity (_Acrobatics_). If you succeed, you and the creature are both restrained until the grapple ends.

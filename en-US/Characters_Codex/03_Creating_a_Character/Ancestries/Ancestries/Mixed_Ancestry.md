# Mixed Ancestry

## Mixed Traits

Your character draws inherited traits from two different ancestral lineages.

**Age.**
You age and mature at roughly the average rate of those lineages.

**Size.**
Your height and weight draw from both lineages. If the lineages use the same size, you use that size; if they differ, choose either size.

**Speed.**
Your base walking speed is the average of the two lineages’ speeds, rounded up to the nearest 5 feet.

Ancestral Traits. Choose one major and one minor ancestral trait from the two ancestries.

* **Major:**
Breath Weapon, Damage Resistance, Dwarven Resilience, Gnome Cunning, Hellish Resistance, Infernal Legacy, It Takes a Village, Lucky
* **Minor:**
Brave, Darkvision, Fey Ancestry, Halfling Nimbleness, Heavy Lifter, Intrepid, Mighty Attacks, Trance

If you want to use ancestries from other sources as one of these lineages, the Conductor’s Companion explains how to determine their major and minor features.

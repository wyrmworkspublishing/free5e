# Level Advancement

As your character gains experience points and levels up, they gain additional features and proficiency bonuses. At 1st level, use the Hit Points at 1st Level entry for your class. Each time you gain a level thereafter, gain one Hit Die and increase your hit point maximum as described by the class whose level you gained: roll that Hit Die or use its fixed value, then add your Constitution modifier. If your Constitution modifier later changes, adjust your hit point maximum by that change for each level you have. The Character Advancement table summarizes the XP and proficiency bonus for each level. Refer to your class description for other improvements.

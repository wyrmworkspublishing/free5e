# Dragonbound

Rooted in reverence for dragons, this culture cherishes diplomacy, ancient knowledge, and legacy.
Its members consider themselves stewards of draconic wisdom and tradition.
\
\
**Draconic Diplomacy.**
You gain [expertise](#expertise) on Charisma checks made to influence dragon creatures.
\
**Dragonbound Teachings.**
You know one cantrip of your choice from the [Cleric](#cleric-spells) or [Wizard spell lists](#wizard-spells).
Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip is Intelligence or Wisdom (whichever is highest).
\
**Progenitor’s Boon.**
Choose one of the following:

* **Chromatic Dragon’s Boon.**
You can cast _<span class="spell spell-Fear_fear">Fear</span>_ without the need for material components once per rest.
Until you reach 5th level, the area of this casting of the spell is limited to a 15-foot cone.
Your spellcasting ability for this spell is Charisma.
* **Essence Dragon’s Boon.**
You know the _<span class="spell spell-Naturecraft_naturecraft">Naturecraft</span>_ cantrip.
In addition, your diplomatic understanding extends towards the creatures of the land and the spirits within it.
You gain [expertise](#expertise) on Charisma checks made to influence beasts and celestials.
* **Gem Dragon’s Boon.**
You know the _<span class="spell spell-Message_message">Message</span>_ cantrip.
Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast _<span class="spell spell-Illusory_Script_illusory_script">Illusory Script</span>_ once per long rest.
At 5th level, you can cast _<span class="spell spell-Imperceptibility_imperceptibility">Imperceptibility</span>_ once per long rest.
You don’t need material components for these spells, and when casting them, your spellcasting ability is Intelligence or Charisma (whichever is highest).
* **Metallic Dragon’s Boon.**
You have [expertise](#expertise) in one of the following skills: _Arcana_, _History_, _Medicine_, _Nature_, or _Religion_.

**Languages.**
You can understand and communicate in Common and Draconic.

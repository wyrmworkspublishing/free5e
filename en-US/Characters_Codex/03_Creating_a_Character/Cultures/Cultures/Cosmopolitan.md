# Cosmopolitan

Urban dwellers from this culture value adaptability, social connections, and quick thinking.
They thrive in diverse environments, seamlessly navigating social circles and leveraging their resourcefulness.
\
\
**Discreetly Armed.**
You gain [expertise](#expertise) on checks made to persuade others to let you remain armed or to conceal weapons or items about your person.
\
**Fashion Sense.**
After you spend at least 1 minute observing a creature within 60 feet, you can use an action to make either a Wisdom (_Insight_) or Intelligence (_History_) check against a DC equal to the creature’s passive Charisma (_Deception_) score.
On a success, you learn the following information about that creature:

* Whether the creature has a lower Charisma score than you.
* A culture, organization, profession, or region suggested by its clothing, insignia, etiquette, or other outward cues.
* The social role or status the creature appears to present within the local community.
* Whether those outward cues appear deliberately misleading, unusual, or out of place.

**Skill Versatility.**
You gain proficiency in _Persuasion_ and one other skill of your choice.
\
**Urban Denizen.**
You can make an Intelligence (_Investigation_) check to learn a person’s location, or gain a helpful clue, by discreetly asking in the right places. The Conductor sets the DC, which is 5 lower for you than it would otherwise be.
\
**Well-Connected.**
You gain an extra contact (see [Backgrounds](#backgrounds)). This person belongs to a different community, profession, organization, district, or social network from your own.
\
**Languages.**
You can understand and communicate in Common and two additional languages.

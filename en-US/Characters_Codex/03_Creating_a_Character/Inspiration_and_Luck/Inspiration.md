# Inspiration

Inspiration is a resource the Conductor may give players to reward certain contributions to the game, such as playing to traits and values, note-taking, creative solutions, or other activities the Conductor considers appropriate. You can have only one Inspiration at a time. This is not Bardic Inspiration—it is not tied to a class and functions differently.

**📌 NOTE**\
Don’t use Inspiration with the [Variant Rule: Luck](#variant-rule:-luck).

# Bounty Hunter

Anyone who knows adventurers knows their legacy is equal parts heroics and havoc: dragons slain, drinking establishments burned to the ground, and merchants robbed. Even those who terrorize others need someone to fear. Rangers who follow the Bounty Hunter path hunt targets wherever they hide and bring them down dead or alive.

> **Repairing Nets**
> \
Nets are heavy and expensive, so a character who relies on them will often want to make repairs. Whether this is possible is up to the Conductor; a reasonable rule is that a net can be repaired in 1 hour with 5 feet of rope, allowing two nets to be repaired during a typical long rest if that is the chosen activity.

## Urban Tracker (3rd Level)

You excel at tracking prey even if they take refuge in the trappings of society.
When you make an Intelligence or Wisdom check related to tracking a creature in cities or towns, your proficiency bonus is doubled if you are using a skill that you’re proficient in.
You can only gain this benefit if you are familiar with the target’s ancestry or general appearance (such as a sketch from a wanted poster).

## Fist of the Law (3rd Level)

You gain proficiency in _Intimidation_.
If you are already proficient in _Intimidation_, you gain [expertise](#expertise) in _Intimidation_, allowing you to add double your proficiency bonus to _Intimidation_ skill checks.

## Dead or Alive (3rd Level)

You can mark a target to bring them down dead or alive.
You can place this mark on a creature you can pinpoint within 120 feet as a bonus action or when you use a bonus action to place another mark on that creature, such as _<span class="spell spell-Hunters_Mark_hunters_mark">Hunter’s Mark</span>_.
This mark lasts for 1 minute, or until you use it again.
Choose one of the following when you mark a creature:

* **Dead.**
When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2.
Any attack roll you make against the marked target is a critical hit on a d20 roll of 19 or 20.
* **Alive.**
When you make an attack roll against the target with a net, you don’t have disadvantage when attacking at melee range or long range.
You can deal nonlethal damage with ranged attacks against the marked target.

## Quick Throw (7th Level)

When you use a net as part of your Attack action, you can make the normal number of attacks, though you can only make one of those attacks with a net.

## Deft Knots (7th Level)

You can add your proficiency bonus to the DC of any check made to escape the restrained condition you inflicted on a creature that you do not already add your proficiency to (for example, tied ropes, shackles, nets, etc.).
Additionally, you have advantage on ability checks to tie knots or secure ropes.

## Pin Down (11th Level)

When a creature you have marked with [Dead or Alive](#dead-or-alive-(3rd-level)) moves 5 feet, stands up from being prone, or escapes from the _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_restrained">restrained</span>_ condition while within 60 feet of you, you can use your reaction to make an opportunity attack against them if they are in range of a weapon you are wielding.
If you make this attack with a ranged weapon, you have disadvantage on the attack.

## Unwavering Pursuit (15th Level)

When you mark a creature with [Dead or Alive](#dead-or-alive-(3rd-level)), you perceive it as though you had [truesight](#truesight) and know its exact location while it is within 300 feet of you. These benefits last until the mark ends.

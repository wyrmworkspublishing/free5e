# Circle of the City

The circle of the city is about adaptation—the adaptation of nature to cities and cities to nature. Wodewoses of this circle recognize the teeming life of a city as another ecosystem adapted to distinctive forms of life. They feel kinship with creatures that remain wild in the city's heart, such as rats and cats. More primeval Wodewoses may view city Wodewoses as little different from other city-dwellers, but members of this circle are no less attuned to nature; they simply perceive a subtler, more pervasive frequency.

## Circle Spells (2nd & 3rd & 5th & 7th & 9th Level)

Your adaptation to the urban environment gives you access to certain avenues of magic that are most helpful for overseeing the interests of nature in such an environment.
At 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to the spells listed for that level in the Circle of the City Spells table.
Once you gain access to one of these spells, you always have it prepared, and it doesn’t count against the number of spells you can prepare each day.
If you gain access to a spell that doesn’t appear on the Wodewose spell list, the spell is nonetheless a Wodewose spell for you.

## Living Eyes of the City (2nd Level)

When you cast _[Animal Friendship](#animal-friendship)_, you can target up to a dozen animals of the same type when targeting Tiny creatures (such as cats, rats, or similar).
When Tiny animals are charmed in this way, they can understand you in a limited capacity, such as recognizing where something is when you describe it in terms they understand, and you can understand them in a limited capacity, such as learning the direction of something. They willingly aid you with simple tasks that do not place them in danger. You can convince them to take greater risks with an ability check involving _Animal Handling_ against a DC set by the Conductor.

Additionally, any familiar you summon with _[Find Familiar](#find-familiar)_ gains the ability to communicate with creatures of a type resembling the form it takes, and has advantage on all _Animal Handling_ checks while interacting with beasts it can communicate with.
As a bonus action, you can expend one use of [Wild Shape](#wild-shape-(2nd-level)) to shift your familiar from one available form to another without resummoning it.

## Controlled Magic (2nd Level)

From operating within the confines of a city, you have managed to tame your magic to more freely operate within the limitations of tight urban environments.
When you cast a spell that targets an area of effect, you can exclude a 10-foot cube from the area of effect (the cube does not have to be entirely within the area of effect).
Alternatively, you can reduce the area of effect of any spell down to any value between its area of effect and a 5-foot radius, and can cast spells that would otherwise not be able to manifest in the environment (such as casting _[Call Lightning](#call-lightning)_ in a room less than 60 feet tall), with your spells adapting to fit within your environment.

## Civilized Beasts (6th Level)

When you use your [Wild Shape](#wild-shape-(2nd-level)) feature to take on the form of a beast, you retain your ability to speak (as long as the beast would normally be able to vocalize sounds at sufficient volume to do so), and become more dexterous and adaptable with paws, claws, or talons, allowing you to use them to perform more complicated tasks that might otherwise require hands.
You additionally retain the ability to cast spells while using Wild Shape to turn into a beast.

**Spellcasting Beasts**
\
The Civilized Beasts feature allows you to cast spells while using Wild Shape, including performing verbal and somatic components. When the beast form can vocalize at sufficient volume, the feature also allows you to speak to your party or perform talking-animal circus acts.

## Urban Adaptation (10th Level)

You become immune to poison and disease, and gain one skill of your choice from _Insight_, _Perception_, _Persuasion_, _Sleight of Hand_, or _Stealth_.
If you have all the listed skills, you can select any skill of your choice instead.

## Keen Skills (14th Level)

You gain proficiency in an additional skill from the list in Urban Adaptation.
Additionally, if a beast you assume the form of is proficient in a skill you are normally proficient in, you gain [expertise](#expertise) in that skill while transformed into that beast.

## Tiny Tributes (14th Level)

Your popularity among the tiny animals of the city reaches an all-time high.
When you complete a long rest within the limits of a city, crows, rats, and other tiny creatures bring you minor treasures.
You can roll on the following table, or your Conductor can determine an appropriate item:

| d6 | Treasure |
| :-: | :-- |
| 1 | 1 probably edible ration |
| 2 | 1d4 copper pieces |
| 3 | 1d4 silver pieces |
| 4 | 1d4 gold pieces |
| 5 | 1 random piece of adventuring gear |
| 6 | 1 platinum piece |

This feature can also provide situationally useful items, such as keys, letters, or other items.

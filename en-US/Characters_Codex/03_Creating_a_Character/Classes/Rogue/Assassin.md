# Assassin

You specialize in the grim art of dealing death.
Those who adhere to this archetype are diverse: assassins, hired killers, spies, bounty hunters and more.

## Bonus Proficiency (3rd Level)

You gain proficiency with your choice of either _Disguise Kit_ or _Poisoner’s Kit_.

## Backstab (3rd Level)

You are pragmatic and deadly in eliminating your foes.
You have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn’t taken a turn in the combat yet, or when an allied creature is within 5 feet of the target and directly opposite you on the target’s other side.

## Killer's Method (9th Level)

You have mastered one technique of your choice for approaching a target.
Select one of the following benefits:

* **False Face.**
You gain proficiency (or [expertise](#expertise) if you already have proficiency) with the _[Disguise Kit](#tools)_, and can assume a prepared disguise with the bonus action granted by [Cunning Action](#cunning-action-(2nd-level)).
* **Blade in the Dark.**
You gain proficiency (or [expertise](#expertise) if you already have proficiency) with the _Stealth_ skill.
You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by dim light.
* **Poisoner’s Path.**
You gain proficiency (or [expertise](#expertise) if you already have proficiency) with the _[Poisoner’s Kit](#tools)_. During a long rest, you can create a long-lasting poison and apply it to one or more weapons at no cost. Until the end of your next long rest, [Sneak Attack](#sneak-attack-(1st-level)) damage dealt with those weapons becomes poison damage, and a creature hit by one of them must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier or be _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_poisoned">poisoned</span>_ for 1 minute. A creature poisoned this way repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on a success. After succeeding on this save, the creature cannot be poisoned by this poison again for 24 hours.

If you gain [expertise](#expertise) in a skill you already have expertise in, you can instead select another skill or tool you have proficiency in to gain expertise in.

## Favored Approach (13th Level)

You hone your skills, gaining a favored approach to dealing death.
Select one of the following benefits:

* **Trusted Face.**
If you spend seven days establishing a fake identity for yourself or three hours studying another person to impersonate, you have advantage on any Charisma (_Deception_) check involving maintaining your disguise and identity.
* **Swift Blade.**
When you roll initiative while Hidden from all hostile creatures rolling initiative, you gain advantage on the roll.
* **Specialized Poisons.**
When you create a poison, choose one creature type (for example, fiends). Against creatures of that type, the poison's damage ignores resistance and immunity to poison damage, and creatures are not immune to the _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_poisoned">poisoned</span>_ condition caused by that poison.

## Lethal Blows (17th Level)

After you deal Sneak Attack damage to a creature, if the target’s remaining hit points are fewer than your Rogue level, the target dies.
You can forgo this when intentionally making nonlethal attacks.

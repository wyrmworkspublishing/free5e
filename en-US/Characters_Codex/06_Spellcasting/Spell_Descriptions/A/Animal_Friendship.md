# Animal Friendship

_1st-level enchantment_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (red ribbon)\
**Duration:** 24 hours

Until the spell ends or you or one of your companions harms it, a beast that you can pinpoint and that has Intelligence 3 or lower is charmed by you unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw. The beast must be able to sense your communication. If you know a sign language, you can prepare the spell to communicate by signing, in which case the beast must be able to see your signs.

**At Higher Levels.**
Choose one additional target for each slot level above 1st.

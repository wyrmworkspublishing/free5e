# Acid Splash

_Conjuration cantrip_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous; unused healing can be distributed for 1 minute

A stinking bubble of acid flies toward up to two creatures within range that are within 5 feet of each other. Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking `1d6` acid damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` when you reach 5th level (`2d6`), 11th level (`3d6`), and 17th level (`4d6`).

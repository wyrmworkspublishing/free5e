# Antipathy/Sympathy

_8th-level enchantment_

**Casting Time:** 1 hour\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (flask of honey and vinegar)\
**Duration:** 10 days

Designate an ancestry of intelligent creatures, such as dragons. A Huge or smaller object or creature, or an area no larger than a 200-foot cube, causes either antipathy or sympathy for those creatures for the duration.

A designated creature that is neither affected by nor immune to this spell must make a Wisdom saving throw when it pinpoints the target or comes within 60 feet of it. On a failed save, it suffers the chosen effect. While affected, it repeats the saving throw every 24 hours and whenever it ends its turn more than 60 feet from the target and unable to pinpoint it, ending the effect on a success. After any successful save, the creature knows it was affected by magic and is immune to this spell for 1 minute.

* **_Antipathy._** While affected, the creature is frightened by the target and must use its movement on each of its turns to move away from it. If the creature moves more than 60 feet from the target and can no longer pinpoint it, the creature is no longer frightened until it can pinpoint the target or comes within 60 feet of it.
* **_Sympathy._** While affected, the creature must use its movement on each of its turns to move into the target’s area or within reach of it and is unwilling to move away. If the target damages or otherwise harms the creature, the creature can immediately repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

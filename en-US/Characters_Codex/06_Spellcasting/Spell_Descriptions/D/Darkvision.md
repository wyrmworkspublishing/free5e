# Darkvision

_2nd-level transmutation_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (either a pinch of dried carrot or an agate)\
**Duration:** 8 hours

One willing creature gains <span class="spell spell-Darkvision_darkvision">darkvision</span> to 60 feet.

**At Higher Levels.**
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the target’s darkvision increases to 120 feet. For each slot level above 3rd, you can target one additional willing creature.

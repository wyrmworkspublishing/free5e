# Darkness

_2nd-level evocation_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, M (bat fur and a drop of pitch or piece of coal)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Magical darkness blocks <span class="spell spell-Darkvision_darkvision">darkvision</span> and nonmagical light in a 15-foot radius sphere.
The darkness spreads around corners, but completely covering or enclosing the source of the darkness with an opaque object blocks the darkness.
If the area of darkness overlaps with magical light created by a spell of 2nd level or lower, the spell that created the light ends.

When cast on an object that is in your possession or unattended, the darkness emanates from that object, moving with it.

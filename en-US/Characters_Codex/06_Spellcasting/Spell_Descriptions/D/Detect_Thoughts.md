# Detect Thoughts

_2nd-level divination_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Self (30-foot radius sphere)\
**Components:** V, S, M (a copper piece)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You can read the thoughts of creatures within 30 feet. As an action, focus on a creature in range. A creature with Intelligence 3 or lower or that knows no language is unaffected; otherwise, you learn its surface thoughts.

As an action, shift to another creature or probe the target. It makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a success, the probe fails but you retain its surface thoughts. On a failure, you learn its reasoning, emotional state, and a significant thought. It knows of the probe and can use an action on its turn to contest your Intelligence check with its own, ending the spell on a tie or win.

Questions to a target shape its thoughts.

As an action, search for thoughts within 30 feet whose sources you can’t pinpoint. The spell is blocked by 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of metal other than lead, or a thin sheet of lead. Once detected, you can then focus on it as an action while it remains in range.

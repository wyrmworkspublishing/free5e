# Locate Animals or Plants

_2nd-level divination (ritual)_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Self (5-mile radius)\
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fur from a bloodhound)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Describe or name a specific kind of beast or plant. Through the voice of nature in your surroundings, you learn the direction and distance to the closest creature or plant of that kind within 5 miles, if any are present.

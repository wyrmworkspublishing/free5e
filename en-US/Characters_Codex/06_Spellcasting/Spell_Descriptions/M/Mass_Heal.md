# Mass Heal
_9th-level evocation_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Healing energy restores up to 700 hit points among any number of creatures you choose within range that are not constructs or undead.
Creatures healed in this way are also cured of any diseases, and any effect causing them to be _[nullified](#nullified)_.
In addition, on subsequent turns within the next minute you can use a bonus action to distribute any unused hit points.

# Bane

_1st-level enchantment_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Up to three creatures of your choice that you can pinpoint must each succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be filled with phantom energies that make them more vulnerable and less capable. Until the spell ends, each target subtracts 1`d4` from its attack rolls and saving throws.

**At Higher Levels.**
You target an additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

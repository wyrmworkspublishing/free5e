# Freedom of Movement

_4th-level abjuration_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap, bound around the arm or a similar appendage)\
**Duration:** 1 hour

One willing creature ignores difficult terrain.
Spells and magical effects can’t reduce its speed or _&lt;&lt;Conditions_paralyzed,&lt;span class="condition>paralyze&lt;/span>>>_ or _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_restrained">restrain</span>_ it.
It can spend 5 feet of movement to escape from nonmagical _restraints_ or _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_grappled">grapples</span>_.
The target's movement and attacks aren't penalized for being [underwater](#underwater-combat).

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th or 7th level, the duration is 8 hours. When you use a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the duration is 24 hours.

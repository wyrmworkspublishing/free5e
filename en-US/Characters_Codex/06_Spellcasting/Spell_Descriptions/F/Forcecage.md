# Forcecage

_7th-level evocation_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 100 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (ruby dust worth 1,500 gp)\
**Duration:** 1 hour

An opaque 10-foot cube of banded force appears centered on a point you can pinpoint within range. Matter and spells cannot pass through it, though creatures can breathe inside it. Each creature wholly within the cube must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a successful save, the creature is pushed to the nearest unoccupied space outside the cube, as is any partially enclosed creature. On a failed save, a creature wholly within the cube is trapped and can’t leave by nonmagical means.

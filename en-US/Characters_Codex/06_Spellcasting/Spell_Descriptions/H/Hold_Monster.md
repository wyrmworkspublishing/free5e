# Hold Monster

_5th-level enchantment_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 90 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a small, straight piece of iron)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

One non-undead creature that you can pinpoint and that fails a Wisdom saving throw is _<span class="condition condition-Conditions_paralyzed">paralyzed</span>_.
At the end of each of its turns, the target makes another saving throw, ending the spell’s effects on it on a successful save.

At Higher Levels. Target one additional creature within 30 feet of the first target for each slot level above 5th. Each additional target must also fail a Wisdom saving throw to be paralyzed.

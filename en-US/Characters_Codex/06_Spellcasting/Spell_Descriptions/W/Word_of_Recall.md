# Word of Recall

_6th-level conjuration_

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 5 feet\
**Components:** V\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You and up to five willing creatures within range instantly teleport to a previously designated sanctuary, appearing in the nearest unoccupied space to the spot you designated when you prepared your sanctuary.

Before using the spell in this way, you must first designate a sanctuary by casting it within a location aligned with your faith, such as a temple of your deity.

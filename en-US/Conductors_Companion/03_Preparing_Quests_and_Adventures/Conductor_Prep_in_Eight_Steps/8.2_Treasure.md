#### Treasure

Piles of coins, shining gems, and powerful relics hidden away await adventurers brave enough to seek them.
Treasure may take the form of currency, gemstones, jewelry, art objects, equipment, consumables, or magic items.
Use treasure to reward risk, reinforce story, and signal what matters in the setting.

Coins are commonly found in denominations of platinum pieces (pp), gold pieces (gp), electrum pieces (ep), silver pieces (sp), and copper pieces (cp).
Fifty coins of any denomination weigh 1 pound.

Treasure need not be uniform.
Slight variation keeps hoards from feeling artificial; for example, two 1,300 gp parcels might instead be 1,145 gp and 1,422 gp.

##### Magic Items by Tier

Use this as a rough expectation, not a limit.
You may follow these guidelines, choose items directly, or roll on the premade magic item tables.

[Magic Items by Tier Table](./Magic_Items_by_Tier_Table.md)

**Guideline:** Expect **one permanent magic item per group every 2–3 levels**, in addition to consumables and single-use relics.
Magic clusters in lairs, vaults, and long-held sites; wandering foes rarely carry much.

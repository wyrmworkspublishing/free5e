#### Step 2: Treasure by Level

Use the following parcels to quickly reward adventuring groups based on the characters’ average level.
Reward **four parcels per level**, or combine parcels to create larger hoards.

[Treasure by Level](./Treasure_by_Level_Table.md)

Coins may be split across denominations or replaced with valuables of equivalent value.
Valuables include gemstones, jewelry, art objects, trade goods, or ceremonial items worth significant coin.
They typically replace part or all of a coin parcel and can be priced at roughly their gold value or used as narrative leverage.
Use the **Mundane Items** table for ideas.

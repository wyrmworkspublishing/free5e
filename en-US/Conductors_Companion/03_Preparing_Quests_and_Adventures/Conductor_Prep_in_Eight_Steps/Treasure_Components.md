##### Treasure Components

| CR    | Not a Lair                      | Lair or Secured Site                                     |
| :----- | :------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–4   | Coins; mundane items            | Coins; valuables; 1 consumable                           |
| 5–10  | Coins; valuables; mundane items | Coins; valuables; 1–2 consumables; weapon or armor       |
| 11–16 | Coins; valuables; consumables   | Coins; valuables; crafted item; single-use magic         |
| 17–20 | Coins; valuables; 1 magic item  | Coins; valuables; 1–2 magic items; story relic           |
| 21+   | Valuables; magic item           | Valuables; multiple magic items; legendary or story item |

Use judgment.
Creatures encountered while traveling usually carry only what they can move or defend.
A lair or secured site reflects time, intent, and control of a location, such as a den, stronghold, vault, shrine, ruin, or hideout, and often contains accumulated or protected wealth.

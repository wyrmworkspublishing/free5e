## Environmental Design
Every environment tells a story.
Weathered ruins, hidden toxins, and unseen forces shape characters’ interactions.
This section provides tools to vividly describe these details and consistently adjudicate their effects.
Use it to turn scenery into story, making places feel alive and responsive to characters’ choices.

### Exploration Encounter Descriptions
<!-- style:sidebar -->
> **Mobility Note** \
> For encounters involving climbing, balancing, or sudden falls, characters using alternate movement—such as prosthetic limbs, wheelchairs, mounts, or magical conveyance—make the same checks adapted to their method of travel (e.g., Animal Handling to control a mount, Athletics to stabilize a wheelchair). Conductors should describe equivalent risks appropriate to that movement type.
>
> * **Acid Field.** Pools emit caustic fumes. _Dexterity saving throw (DC 14)_ or take 2d6 acid damage; a successful _Intelligence (Nature)_ check _(DC 13)_ locates safe footing.
> * **Bridge of Sorrow / Stones.** A crumbling bridge spans a void. Make a _Dexterity (Acrobatics)_ check (DC 14) to cross and/or a _Strength (Athletics)_ check (DC 12) to assist others.
> * **Caught in the Crossfire.** Rival factions clash nearby. A successful _Dexterity saving throw (DC 15)_ avoids stray attacks; a successful _Charisma (Persuasion)_ check _(DC 13)_ may de-escalate or negotiate passage.
> * **Choking Smoke.** Burning debris fills the air. Make a _Constitution saving throw (DC 14)_ each minute or gain one level of exhaustion. The area is lightly obscured within 10 ft. and heavily obscured beyond that.
> * **Collapsing Roof.** A structure gives way above. Make a _Dexterity saving throw (DC 14)_ to avoid 2d6 bludgeoning damage and/or a _Strength (Athletics)_ check (DC 10) to rescue trapped victims.
> * **Counterfeit Goods.** Merchants sell falsified or stolen wares to the party. A successful _Intelligence (Investigation)_ or _Wisdom (Insight)_ check (DC 12–15) reveals the deception; failure leads to the item breaking when used.
> * **Cursed Temple.** Defiled ground radiates magical corruption. Make a _Wisdom saving throw (DC 15)_ or become _frightened_ for 1 minute; a successful _Intelligence (Religion) (DC 13)_ identifies the curse’s origin.
> * **Dark Alleys.** Twisting passages hide thieves and worse. Succeed on a _Wisdom (Survival)_ or _Intelligence (Investigation)_ check (DC 13) to navigate them; a failed check risks an ambush or getting lost for 1d4 hours.
> * **Dense Fog.** Thick mist fills the area, making it _lightly obscured_. Creatures within have _impaired (sight)_; those relying on sight take a –1 penalty on attack rolls per 10 feet to the target.
> * **Enchanted Statue.** A pressure plate triggers a cone of elemental magic. Perception (DC 17) spots the trap; Dexterity (thieves’ tools) DC 14 disables it. On failure, each creature in a 15-ft. cone makes a Dexterity saving throw (DC 14) or takes 13 (2d12) damage of the statue’s element (acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison).
> * **Endless Plummet.** A shaft drops into endless dark. Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) avoids falling. A creature that falls can make a Strength (Athletics) check (DC 12) each round to catch a ledge before impact, taking normal falling damage (1d6 per 10 ft., up to 20d6) on failure.
> * **Falling Net.** A net trap drops from above, usually sprung by thieves or bounty hunters. Succeed on a _Dexterity saving throw (DC 13)_ to avoid becoming _restrained_; a successful _Strength (Athletics)_ check (DC 10) (by a captured creature or someone else) ends the condition.
> * **Flash Flood.** A sudden torrent sweeps through the area. Succeed on a Strength (Athletics) check (DC 13) to stay upright or grab hold; failure carries the creature away and deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. Creatures with a swim speed or climb speed make the check with advantage.
> * **Forge, Foundry, and Factory.** Roaring furnaces, molten channels, and grinding gears fill this space. Group Dexterity (Acrobatics), Wisdom (Perception), or Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check (DC 15) to cross safely or avoid attention from workers. Failure causes 4 (1d8) fire damage or minor burns, imposing disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks until a short rest. Creatures wearing medium or heavy armor must also succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 14) or gain a level of exhaustion from the heat.
> * **God Corpse.** Massive divine remains radiate necrotic energy. Make a _Constitution saving throw_ (DC 16) each round or take 2d10 necrotic damage.
> * **Hail Storm.** Sudden ice pelts the area, cracking glass and muffling sound. A successful Dexterity saving throw (DC 12) avoids 1d4 bludgeoning damage. Creatures in the storm have impaired (sight, hearing) while it lasts; those relying on sight take a –1 penalty on attack rolls per 20 feet to the target.
> * **Haze.** Fine dust, smoke, or spores thicken the air. Creatures must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 13) each minute or begin coughing, granting disadvantage on Concentration checks and Stealth until clear air is found. Creatures in the area have impaired (sight, parasense: audible echolocation, sporolocation) until the haze settles.
> * **Killing Cloud.** A lethal magical vapor sweeps the streets. Make a _Constitution saving throw (DC 16)_ each round or take 2d10 poison damage and be _poisoned_ for 1 minute.
> * **Labyrinthine Ravines.** Maze-like channels mislead travelers. Succeed on a _Wisdom (Survival)_ check (DC 14) or become lost for 1d4 hours.
> * **Lethal Outgassing.** Heavy, toxic vapors seep from vents and decay, clouding thought and dulling senses. While in the gas, creatures must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) at the start of each turn or suffer the effects of the confusion spell until the start of their next turn. Creatures immune to poison or the poisoned condition are unaffected. Areas of outgassing are lightly obscured, and creatures within have impaired (sight, parasense: baroreception, chemoreception) until the gas disperses.
> * **Lost Item.** A dropped satchel, coin purse, or trinket lies nearby — anyone attentive spots it automatically. A Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Investigation) check (DC 13) reveals its likely owner or whether it’s bait for a trap. Returning it may earn goodwill; keeping it risks pursuit or accusation.
> * **Magical Overgrowth.** Enchanted fungi release mind-warping spores. Creatures that start their turn in the area must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 13) or become distracted until the end of their next turn. While distracted, a creature can’t take reactions and has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. The area is lightly obscured, and creatures within have impaired (sight, parasense: sporolocation) until the spores settle.
> * **Malfunctioning Planar Portal.** Unstable planar energies lash outward from the portal in unpredictable bursts. Each creature within 10 feet of the portal must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 16) or take 3d10 force damage from a surge of wild energy. A creature adjacent to the portal can attempt an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 14) to stabilize it for 1 minute, after which travel through is safe.
> * **Marsh Gas.** Flammable vapors fill the area. Succeed at a _Wisdom (Perception)_ or Intelligence (_Investigation)_ check (DC 13) to detect by smell, vibration, or air pressure; lighting a magical or non-magical flame triggers a 10 ft. radius explosion causing 2d6 fire.
> * **Perilous Cliff Path.** A narrow ledge winds above a deep chasm. Creatures must succeed on a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check (DC 14) to keep their footing or fall 60 feet. A creature that falls must succeed on a Strength (Athletics) check (DC 12) to climb back up. Creatures with a climbing speed automatically succeed on these checks.
> * **Pests.** Vermin overrun a cellar or market stall. A success on a Wisdom (_Animal Handling) or Intelligence (Nature) check (DC 10)_ calms them; _Attacks_ or fire may escalate to combat against a swarm (as a Swarm of Rats).
> * **Pit Trap.** Concealed drop 10 ft. deep. Succeed on a _Wisdom (Perception)_ check (DC 13) to spot the trap; if activated, make a _Dexterity saving throw_ (DC 13) or take 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
> * **Poorly-Repaired Tunnel.** Unstable ceiling rains debris. Make a _Dexterity saving throw_ (DC 12) to avoid 1d6 bludgeoning damage; repeated stress risks collapse.
> * **Primordial Tornado.** Elemental winds tear through the area, hurling debris and crushing structures. Creatures must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 17) each round or take 3d6 bludgeoning damage and be pushed 20 feet in a random direction. A creature can brace itself with a Strength (Athletics) check (DC 17) to avoid being moved. Flight is impossible within 100 feet. The area imposes impaired (sight, parasense: baroreception, chemoreception) until the storm passes.
> * **Private Property.** The party enters a restricted area. Succeed on a Dexterity (_Stealth)_ check (DC 13) to avoid discovery; on a failure guards are alerted and will arrive within 1d4 minutes.
> * **Public Ceremony.** Crowds block streets for a festival or funeral. Succeed on a _Dexterity (Acrobatics)_ check (DC 10) to move through; or succeed on a _Charisma (Persuasion)_ or _Charisma (Intimidation)_ check (DC 12) to part the crowd.
> * **Quicksand.** Subterranean mud pulls creatures down. Make a _Strength saving throw_ (DC 13) or become _restrained_; succeed on a _Dexterity (Athletics) check (DC 12)_ to escape.
> * **Rockfall.** Loose stone collapses. Make a _Dexterity saving throw_ (DC 14) to avoid 2d6 bludgeoning damage.
> * **Rolling Sphere.** Runaway boulder or relic. Make a _Dexterity saving throw_ (DC 15) or take 3d6 bludgeoning damage; or succeed on a _Constitution (Athletics) check (DC 13) to_ outrun it.
> * **Rooftop Run.** Chase or escape over the city’s heights. Succeed on a _Strength (Athletics)_ or _Dexterity (Acrobatics)_ check (DC 14) to jump or balance; three failed checks cause a 2d6 bludgeoning or piercing damage (depending on where you land) as you fall from the rooftop.
> * **Rot Grubs.** Flesh-eating larvae lurk in corpses or refuse. A creature disturbing the area must succeed on a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check (DC 12) to avoid contact. On a failure, the grubs infest the creature. Any amount of fire damage kills exposed grubs. An infected creature can be cured with either fire damage or a successful Intelligence (Medicine) check (DC 15) to remove the burrowing larvae.
> * **Sinkhole.** The ground collapses beneath your feet. Creatures must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 14) to avoid falling. A creature that falls must succeed on a Strength (Athletics) check (DC 12) to climb out. Creatures with a climbing speed automatically succeed on the climbing check.
> * **Sphere of Annihilation.** A silent black void drifts through the air, bending light and pulling nearby matter toward it. A creature within 30 feet feels the disturbance and must succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 18) to recognize the danger; creatures with parasense (magoreception, baroreception) make the check with advantage. Any creature or object that touches the sphere is instantly destroyed, as by the Disintegrate spell.
> * **Spinning Walls.** Hidden mechanisms silently rotate sections of corridor. Creatures must succeed on a Wisdom (Perception) check (DC 14) to notice faint motion or shifting air; those with parasense: baroreception or kinetoreception have advantage on this check. A creature that succeeds on an Intelligence (Investigation) check (DC 13) identifies how to operate or disable the controls.
> * **Stampede / Thundering Stampede.** Fleeing beasts crush the tunnel. Make a _Dexterity saving throw_ (DC 14) or take 3d6 bludgeoning damage.
> * **Sunspots.** Solar or arcane flares surge in rhythmic bursts, distorting light and magical stability. Creatures must succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 15) to anticipate the pattern. Those that succeed have advantage on concentration checks until the next flare; all others make concentration checks with disadvantage. The area is lightly obscured, and creatures relying on sight have impaired (sight) while the flares persist. Creatures using nonvisual senses are unaffected.
> * **Tornado.** Cyclonic winds tear through the area, scattering debris and crushing weak structures. Creatures must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 16) or be knocked prone and pushed up to 20 feet. Unsecured objects weighing less than 100 pounds are flung away and likely destroyed. Those up to 500 pounds are displaced, and larger structures may lose roofs, windows, or exterior walls at the Conductor’s discretion. Trees and reinforced buildings generally remain standing but may be damaged. Flight is impossible within 100 feet.
> * **Urban Blaze.** Fire races through tightly packed buildings, filling the air with choking smoke. Creatures must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 13) each round or take 1d6 fire damage. A successful Wisdom (Survival) check (DC 14) locates a safe escape route. The area is heavily obscured, and creatures relying on smell or parasense: chemoreception have impaired (smell) while the smoke persists.
> * **Urban Quake.** Tremors shake the ground, toppling walls and opening fissures. Creatures must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) or fall prone and take 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage from falling debris. On a success, a creature keeps its footing and avoids harm. Creatures relying on tremorsense or parasense: baroreception or kinetoreception have those senses impaired while the quake continues.
> * **Voracious Pests.** Swarms devour crops, cloth, or books. Creatures must succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (Nature) check (DC 15)\* to identify the magical cause; fire or loud noise disperses the swarm in 1d4 rounds. If attacked, treat the pests as a Swarm of Insects or a thematically appropriate variant.

### Monuments
Filling the various chambers and locations in your game with interesting features is always a challenge — and can be even more difficult to improvise.
The following four "Monuments" lists can help you generate fantastic features with a number of potential effects.
You don't need to roll on every table each time you want to generate a feature.
Sometimes, just establishing the condition and the type of a feature is enough to give you a starting point.
Then you can let the story or even the background of one of the characters guide the feature's additional elements.

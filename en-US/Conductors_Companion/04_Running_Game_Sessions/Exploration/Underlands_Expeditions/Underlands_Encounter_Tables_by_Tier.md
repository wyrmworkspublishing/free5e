## 8. Select Rewards

Belowground rewards focus on discovery and access in addition to gold and gems.

- **Access:** safe routes, maps, or sanctuary rights.  
- **Resources:** rare minerals, spores, or resonant crystals.  
- **Allies:** guides, trade partners, or trustworthy enclaves.  
See **Rewards** in _Conducting Game Sessions_ for conversion into tangible benefits.

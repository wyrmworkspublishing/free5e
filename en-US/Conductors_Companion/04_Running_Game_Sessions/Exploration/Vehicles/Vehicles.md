## Vehicles

All vehicles have the following properties:

**Terrain.**
Vehicles fall into three terrain categories: land, water, and air.
They travel in their respective terrain.
Proficiency in a vehicle category grants a creature a bonus to checks made with that type.

**Size and Armor Class.**
Like creatures, vehicles have size categories, and each has an AC that determines how difficult it is to hit.

**Hit Points.**
A vehicle is destroyed when reduced to 0 hit points or fewer.

**Speed.**
Vehicles are listed with their movement and long-distance travel speeds.

**Crew.**
Many vehicles need only one driver, but larger ones often require dozens to operate properly.
Drivers can’t use a vehicle without meeting the minimum crew requirement.

**Immunities.**
Vehicles are immune to poison and psychic damage and to the <span class="condition condition-Conditions_charmed">charmed</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_exhausted">exhausted</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_frightened">frightened</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_impaired">impaired</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_incapacitated">incapacitated</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_nullified">nullified</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_paralyzed">paralyzed</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_petrified">petrified</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_poisoned">poisoned</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_prone">prone</span>, <span class="condition condition-Conditions_stunned">stunned</span>, and <span class="condition condition-Conditions_unconscious">unconscious</span> conditions, and any effect that would result from failing a Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma saving throw.

**Saving Throws.**
Unlike most objects, vehicles must make Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws as if they were creatures against effects that call for them.
Vehicles have a Strength and Constitution based on their size.
A driven vehicle has a Dexterity score equal to its driver’s.
A vehicle without a driver has a Dexterity of 0.

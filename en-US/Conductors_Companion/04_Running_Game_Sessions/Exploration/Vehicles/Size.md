### Size

A vehicle’s size determines its Strength, Constitution, and carrying capacity.

**Large.**
A Large vehicle has a Strength and Constitution of 14 (+2).
It can carry up to 3 passengers and driver and 2,000 pounds of carrying capacity.

**Huge.**
A Huge vehicle has a Strength and Constitution of 18 (+4).
It can carry up to 6 passengers and driver and 4,000 pounds of carrying capacity.

**Gargantuan.**
A Gargantuan vehicle has a Strength and Constitution score of 22 (+6).
It can carry a number of additional passengers equal to the minimum required crew and 40,000 pounds of carrying capacity.

**Collisions.**
A collision occurs when a moving vehicle enters the space of a creature or object.
The vehicle and the impacted creature or object take bludgeoning damage based on the vehicle’s size: Large—4d6, Huge—8d6, Gargantuan—12d6.
If the impacted creature or object is the same size or larger, the vehicle stops.
If smaller, it’s shunted to the nearest unoccupied space that allows the vehicle to move.

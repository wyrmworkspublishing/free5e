## Vehicles
All vehicles have the following properties:

**Terrain.** Vehicles fall into three terrain categories: land, water, and air.
They travel in their respective terrain.
Proficiency in a vehicle category grants a creature a bonus to checks made with that type.
**Size and Armor Class.** Like creatures, vehicles have size categories, and each has an AC that determines how difficult it is to hit.
**Hit Points.** A vehicle is destroyed when reduced to 0 hit points or fewer.
**Speed.** Vehicles are listed with their movement and long-distance travel speeds.
**Crew.** Many vehicles need only one driver, but larger ones often require dozens to operate properly.
Drivers can’t use a vehicle without meeting the minimum crew requirement.
**Immunities.** Vehicles are immune to poison and psychic damage and to the charmed, exhausted, frightened, impaired, incapacitated, nullified, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, stunned, and unconscious conditions, and any effect that would result from failing a Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma saving throw.
**Saving Throws.** Unlike most objects, vehicles must make Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws as if they were creatures against effects that call for them.
Vehicles have a Strength and Constitution based on their size.
A driven vehicle has a Dexterity score equal to its driver’s,.
A vehicle without a driver has a Dexterity of 0.

### Size
A vehicle’s size determines its Strength, Constitution, and Carrying Capacity.

**Large.** A Large vehicle has a Strength and Constitution of 14 (+2).
It can carry up to 3 passengers and driver and 2,000 pounds of carrying capacity.
**Huge.** A Huge vehicle has a Strength and Constitution of 18 (+4).
It can carry up to 6 passengers and driver and 4,000 pounds of carrying capacity.
**Gargantuan.** A Gargantuan vehicle has a Strength and Constitution score of 22 (+6).
It can carry a number of additional passengers equal to the minimum required crew and 40,000 pounds of carrying capacity.

**Collisions**.
A collision occurs when a moving vehicle enters the space of a creature or object.
The vehicle and the impacted creature or object take bludgeoning damage based on the vehicle’s size: Large—4d6, Huge—8d6, Gargantuan—12d6.
If the impacted creature or object is the same size or larger, the vehicle stops.
If smaller, it’s shunted to the nearest unoccupied space that allows the vehicle to move.

### Special Features
Vehicles can have many fantastical features, but the most common are:

**Armed**.
Armed vehicles carry one or more siege weapons (see Table: Siege Weaponry).
A creature can use an action to fire or reload a siege weapon.
**Drawn**.
A drawn vehicle is pulled by one or more beasts of burden, and its speed depends on the animals pulling it.
**Personal**.
Personal vehicles are designed for a single occupant and have less space.
They can only carry one person and have a quarter of the carrying capacity of a normal vehicle of the same size.
**Three-dimensional**.
Usually found on airships, a three-dimensional vehicle may also turn up or down when making turns.
**Transport**.
Transport vehicles are designed for cargo and crew, with twice the carrying capacity and maximum number of passengers as normal for their size.

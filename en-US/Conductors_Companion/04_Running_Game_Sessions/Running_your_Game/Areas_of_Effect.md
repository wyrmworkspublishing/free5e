#### Areas of Effect
The following guidelines offer a rough baseline for determining the number of Medium targets that fall within an area of effect.
The circumstances of a battle can increase or decrease this number.
For example, for larger monsters or monsters that are more spread out, an area of effect might target only half the normal number of creatures.
For smaller monsters or monsters that are packed close together, an area of effect might include up to double the normal number of targets.

- **Tiny Area:** 1 or 2 creatures (_<span class="spell spell-Cloud_of_Daggers_cloud_of_daggers">Cloud of Daggers</span>_)
- **Small Area:** 2 creatures (_<span class="spell spell-Burning_Hands_burning_hands">Burning Hands</span>_, _<span class="spell spell-Thunderwave_thunderwave">Thunderwave</span>_)
- **Large Area:** 4 creatures (_<span class="spell spell-Cone_of_Cold_cone_of_cold">Cone of Cold</span>_, _<span class="spell spell-Fireball_fireball">Fireball</span>_)
- **Huge Area:** Everyone (_<span class="spell spell-Circle_of_Death_circle_of_death">Circle of Death</span>_, _<span class="spell spell-Earthquake_earthquake">Earthquake</span>_)
- **Short Line:** 2 creatures (_<span class="spell spell-Wall_of_Fire_wall_of_fire">Wall of Fire</span>_)
- **Long Line:** 3 creatures (_<span class="spell spell-Blade_Barrier_blade_barrier">Blade Barrier</span>_, _<span class="spell spell-Lightning_Bolt_lightning_bolt">Lightning Bolt</span>_)

An area of effect that targets both the characters and their enemies might affect more total targets, including an effect created by a character or monster willing to put allies in harm's way.
If a spellcaster character wishes to place allies within an area of effect, you and the players should negotiate this before the spell is cast.

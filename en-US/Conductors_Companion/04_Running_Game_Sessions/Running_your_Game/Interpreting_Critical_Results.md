#### Interpreting Critical Results
When a player rolls a **natural 20** or **natural 1** on an ability check, you can treat it as a **critical success** or **critical failure** if doing so adds to the moment.
A critical result stays within what is actually possible: a natural 20 represents the best plausible success, not an impossible outcome, while a natural 1 represents the worst plausible failure without creating an arbitrary catastrophe.

- A **critical success** might bring precision, speed, or an impressive extra result.
- A **critical failure** still fails, but it should add tension or surprise.
- Critical results are story beats—use them to keep the action moving and the table smiling.
